Slate Calc Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Slate Calc does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your calculations, history, memories and settings are saved only on your device, so they are still there when you reopen the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
