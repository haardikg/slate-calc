Slate Calc Support

Need help or found a bug? Email haardikgupta13@gmail.com and include your device and what you pressed.

Where is my history?

Tap the clock button above the keypad. Tap an entry to load it; its result becomes ANS. Swipe left on an entry to delete it.

How do I change the theme?

Tap the palette button at the end of the mode line and pick Graphite, Light or Midnight.

How do I switch between fractions and decimals?

Press CHANGE after a result. It cycles through the improper fraction, mixed number and decimal.

How do I reset everything?

The small RESET button above 2nd F resets settings. Your history and theme are kept.
